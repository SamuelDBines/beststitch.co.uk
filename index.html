Questions

Alterations in Bromley: common questions

How much does it cost to shorten trousers or jeans?

Shortening plain trousers or jeans starts from £14. Shorten and turn up starts from £16. See the price list above for the rest.

Do you alter wedding and bridesmaid dresses?

Yes. We alter evening, bridesmaid and wedding dresses. Bring the dress in or call 020 3689 8745 for a quotation.

Can you make or shorten curtains?

Yes. We make curtains to measure (price on application) and shorten curtains from £10 per metre width.

How much is a new zip?

Jean, trouser and skirt zips start from £14, dress and invisible zips from £18, and anorak zips from £25.

Do you do dress making and embroidery?

Yes. Dress making and embroidery are both on our list of services. Call 020 3689 8745 or pop in to talk it through.
